A simple wordmark proposal.

This original, one-color SVG wordmark is approved for the modernist direction. It is shown at a 108px navigation width and at a smaller 52px width. It is not yet part of the storefront.

NAVIGATION SIZE / ONE COLOR

MIN / HOME

SMALL SIZE / SAME SVG

MIN / MENU

What to review

  • Silhouette: open, geometric strokes spell MIN.
  • Readability: the letters are uppercase and spaced clearly.
  • Contrast: the same single-color SVG is shown dark on warm paper and white on dark ink.
  • Risk: the open strokes may feel technical or familiar rather than distinctive.

This wordmark is selected for the brand. Its visual review does not establish trademark clearance.
