00 / Direction

Decisions first.

Record your agreed reasoning in SPEC.md, then reflect it here.

Audience is a working hypothesis

Audience

People who do not know what else to wear and want a simple shirt to throw on.

Need: an easy default when getting dressed feels uncertain. Not validated.

Secondary hypothesis: someone looking for a plain layer under other clothes. Not validated.

Archetype & voice

Innocent

Calm, reassuring, reliable.

Visual direction

Modernist, clean and minimal with a soft, warm feel.

Promise: “Just good old reliable for when you don't know what to wear”.

06 / Voice

Words are
design, too.

Calm, reassuring, and reliable. Keep language grounded in what is known.

Promise

“Just good old reliable for when you don't know what to wear.”

Voice traits: calm, reassuring, reliable.

Support

“A reliable t-shirt for your times of uncertainty.”

07 / Helpful details

Make choosing
feel lighter.

Two approved ideas to help an uncertain visitor.

A small styling tip

“A simple start: pair the white T-shirt with something you already like wearing.”

A free useful suggestion offers a place to begin, without asking for anything in return.

Shared need

“For anyone who wants getting dressed to feel simpler.”

Names a shared goal without implying popularity or making a product claim.

These are design hypotheses, not tested customer outcomes. The secondary audience is a hypothesis, not a validated customer need.
